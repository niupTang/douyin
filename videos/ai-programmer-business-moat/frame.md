# Editorial AI Field Manual

Concept angle: a programmer crosses from a shrinking code factory into a growing business-results machine. The visual language is tactile editorial paper collage: black-and-white halftone cut-outs, warm cream keylines, flat color fields, registration marks, paper grain and mechanical snap-in assembly.

- Focal element: one oversized metaphor on the left 60% of the vertical frame.
- Edge anchors: act label at top-left, thin registration rule at bottom-left, scene number near the inner gutter.
- Supporting detail: 2–4 paper cards, cables, arrows or stamps that clarify cause and effect.
- Background: flat strong color field with subtle paper fiber; no glossy gradients.
- Presenter safe zone: rightmost 35% remains visually quiet from y=260 to y=1680, outlined only in preview with a removable dashed guide.
- Typography: Archivo Black for short Chinese emphasis through system CJK fallback; IBM Plex Mono for API/FDE/AI labels and numerals.
- Motion: assemble from empty using `waterfall-entry`, `spring-pop-entrance`, `svg-path-draw`, `depth-scatter-assemble`; no slow zoom.
